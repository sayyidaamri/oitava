The Ericeira Guide

A Fishing Town That Surfs

Ericeira is a town first and a resort second. White lanes trimmed in blue run down to a harbour that still works, and a protected stretch of coast holds some of the best waves in Europe. Owners come all year, which is rare.

Illustrated map, not to scale

    The old town is compact and loud in summer, calm from October. North is for surfers, south is for families, and inland towards Mafra you trade the view for a garden.

    Houses here are small and stairs are steep. We look for roof terraces, dry walls and somewhere sensible to keep eight wetsuits.
