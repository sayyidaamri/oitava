The Tavira Guide

The Quiet End of the Algarve

Tavira sits on a tidal river behind a line of barrier islands. Roofs are four-sided, lunch is slow and the beaches need a boat, which keeps them calm. The water is the warmest in the country and winter barely exists.

    The eastern Algarve never grew upwards. Between Santa Luzia and Cacela Velha you find low white houses, salt pans full of flamingos and a lagoon that changes colour through the day.

    We like houses with a roof terrace and a courtyard: one for winter sun, one for August shade. Most of ours are a short walk from a ferry.
