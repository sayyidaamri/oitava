Comporta

Rice fields, pines and a very long beach

The Comporta Guide

Dunes First, Houses Second

Comporta keeps its buildings low and its horizon long. Villages sit behind the dunes among umbrella pines and rice paddies, and most errands can be done by bicycle. It suits owners who want space, sand and early nights.

Illustrated map, not to scale

    Comporta runs from the Sado estuary down to the lagoon at Melides, and each village has its own pace: storks and shops in Comporta itself, pine shade at Carvalhal, quiet farm tracks inland.

    Building rules are strict here, so good houses rarely come up. We walk every plot before we buy, usually in August, when the wind and the parking tell the truth.
