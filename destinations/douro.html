The Douro Valley Guide

A River Instead of a Sea

Not every owner wants sand. The Douro is terraces, orchards and a wide calm river, with warm evenings into October. Houses here are older, walls are thicker and neighbours bring grapes.

    The valley was one of the first wine regions in the world to be drawn on a map, and the old estates still set the rhythm: pruning in winter, flowers in spring, harvest in September.
