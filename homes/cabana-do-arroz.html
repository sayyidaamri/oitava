About This House

Pale timber inside and out, with a deck that looks straight over the rice. The house has 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms across 145 square metres, and was chosen for the way it works with a full table: somewhere to gather, somewhere to disappear, and enough hot water for both.

Comporta keeps its buildings low and its horizon long. Villages sit behind the dunes among umbrella pines and rice paddies, and most errands can be done by bicycle. It suits owners who want space, sand and early nights.

Our team renewed wiring, linen and kitchen equipment before the first owners arrived. Each of the eight owners has a lockable cupboard, a set of rotating weeks and one vote. 3 of 8 shares are placed, so the house is already running, with a manager, a budget and a calendar you can see before you decide.
