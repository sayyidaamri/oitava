This is a static demo, so nothing was sent. On a live site, Inês would reply with an estimate per eighth, usually the same day.

Selling a family house is personal. You can keep one eighth, or three, and release the rest. The shutters stay open, the village keeps its neighbours, and you still get July now and then.

Priced by the Eighth

Automated estimates miss what moves a Portuguese coastal house: damp, deeds, access and distance to a swim. We price the whole from recent sales, then divide it openly.

Ready for Eight Families

Tired kitchen, full attic, uncertain wiring. We fund the works and manage the builders, so the house is ready before the first owner arrives.

Keep Your House Manager

If someone already looks after the place, we would like to meet them. Several of our best house managers came with the keys.

A List of Waiting Owners

Your shares go first to people who have already told us where they want to be, which coast they swim on and how many children come along.

About shares and prices

Information on this site is prepared with care but is not guaranteed. Each share is an undivided one-eighth interest in the company that holds a single house. Share prices include purchase taxes paid by the holding company, works and furniture, and exclude personal notary fees. PLEASE TAKE INDEPENDENT LEGAL AND TAX ADVICE BEFORE YOU COMMIT. Weeks, running costs and resale values shown are estimates based on current budgets and may change. Photographs may not be reproduced without permission.

Fair Ownership & Equal Access

We welcome owners regardless of nationality, origin, religion, gender, orientation, age, disability or family situation. Every owner in a house holds the same share, the same vote and the same number of weeks.
