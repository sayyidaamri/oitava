About shares and prices

Information on this site is prepared with care but is not guaranteed. Each share is an undivided one-eighth interest in the company that holds a single house. Share prices include purchase taxes paid by the holding company, works and furniture, and exclude personal notary fees. PLEASE TAKE INDEPENDENT LEGAL AND TAX ADVICE BEFORE YOU COMMIT. Weeks, running costs and resale values shown are estimates based on current budgets and may change. Photographs may not be reproduced without permission.

Fair Ownership & Equal Access

We welcome owners regardless of nationality, origin, religion, gender, orientation, age, disability or family situation. Every owner in a house holds the same share, the same vote and the same number of weeks.
