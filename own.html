One Registered Eighth

Own a Share

Whether this is your first place by the sea or a smaller, saner version of the one you have, owning well comes down to clarity. We set out the weeks, the costs and the exit before you fall for the view.

Search Portugal

Find Your Eighth

Six Weeks, Fairly Dealt

The year is divided into eight equal sets of weeks that rotate, so every owner gets a turn at Easter and August. Swap, lend or leave yours empty. Nobody needs to be persuaded.

One Budget, Split Eight Ways

Cleaning, garden, insurance, repairs and the house manager sit in one yearly budget that all eight owners approve. You see every invoice, and you pay an eighth of it.

Designed to Be Shared

Rita draws every house for many hands: eight lockable cupboards, two good showers more than you would expect, and chairs that survive wet swimsuits.

Yours to Keep or Pass On

Your eighth is registered in your name. Leave it to your children or sell it whenever you like: the other owners get first refusal, then our list of waiting buyers.

About shares and prices

Information on this site is prepared with care but is not guaranteed. Each share is an undivided one-eighth interest in the company that holds a single house. Share prices include purchase taxes paid by the holding company, works and furniture, and exclude personal notary fees. PLEASE TAKE INDEPENDENT LEGAL AND TAX ADVICE BEFORE YOU COMMIT. Weeks, running costs and resale values shown are estimates based on current budgets and may change. Photographs may not be reproduced without permission.

Fair Ownership & Equal Access

We welcome owners regardless of nationality, origin, religion, gender, orientation, age, disability or family situation. Every owner in a house holds the same share, the same vote and the same number of weeks.
