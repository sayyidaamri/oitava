The Owner Exchange and Seasonal Weeks

By Private Swap

More Than One Front Door

Alongside your own house you can stay in all the others. Put a week you will not use into the Exchange and take one somewhere else: harvest in the Douro, surf in Ericeira, oranges in Tavira.

This is not a points scheme. Every swap is a week for a week, arranged by a person who knows both houses, so you have one name to call from first idea to last check-out.

Week for Week, Start to Finish

How It Works

  1. 01

    Offer a Week

    Tell us which of your weeks you will not use. Rough dates are enough, and it costs nothing to ask.

  2. 02

    We Find the Match

    We check the other calendars, confirm what is really free and send two or three options.

  3. 03

    You Choose, We Prepare

    Pick a house. We brief its manager on allergies, bedtimes and how you take your coffee.

  4. 04

    Both Houses Reset

    Cleaners in, keys checked, calendars updated. Same contact throughout, so nothing gets lost.

Information on this site is prepared with care but is not guaranteed. Each share is an undivided one-eighth interest in the company that holds a single house. Share prices include purchase taxes paid by the holding company, works and furniture, and exclude personal notary fees. PLEASE TAKE INDEPENDENT LEGAL AND TAX ADVICE BEFORE YOU COMMIT. Fair Ownership & Equal Access

We welcome owners regardless of nationality, origin, religion, gender, orientation, age, disability or family situation. Every owner in a house holds the same share, the same vote and the same number of weeks.
